Fangyun Wei

I am a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia. My research spans visual generative models, multimodal learning, embodied intelligence, and data-efficient learning.

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computer Science, Faculty of Engineering, at the University of Sydney, under the supervision of Prof. Chang Xu. I earned my master's degree in Intelligence Science and Technology from the School of EECS at Peking University and my bachelor's degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Shandong University.

I serve as an Area Chair for ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, and AAAI.
